# Ars Warp to Map: Xaero's

Shows the destination of your Ars Nouveau Warp Scrolls as Xaero's Minimap waypoints.

Carry a Warp Scroll or Stable Warp Scroll and its recorded destination appears on
Xaero's Minimap as a waypoint. Move the scroll out of your inventory and the waypoint
is removed. When a scroll has a custom (anvil) name, the waypoint uses it.

Everything is read on the client from your own inventory, so the mod works the same in
single-player, LAN, and on servers. It only looks at scrolls you are carrying. Chests
and ender chests are not scanned.

## Requirements

- [Ars Nouveau](https://modrinth.com/mod/ars-nouveau) for the warp scrolls
- [Xaero's Minimap](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-minimap) on the client to display waypoints

Supported platforms:

- Minecraft 1.21.1 — NeoForge 21.1.x
- Minecraft 1.20.1 — Forge 47.x

Ars Nouveau and Xaero's Minimap are both optional for loading the mod; without them it
simply does nothing. Waypoints appear for the dimension you are currently in; scrolls
pointing at another dimension, and waypoints that should be removed there, are applied
when you next enter that dimension.
